Diversity
---

Diversity is the differences in backgrounds, experiences, expertise, and perspectives. Understanding our team's strengths and gaps helps us identify assumptions that could affect our product.


Team Background
---

Our team has a strong technical background, with all three members studying Computer Science at UofT. Our combined experience covers machine learning, statistics, risk analytics, backend engineering, computer vision, computer architecture, and ML systems.

## Strengths & Weaknesses

**Strengths**: Our main strength is our ability to understand AI technology, analyze data, and quickly build and test technical solutions. 

**Weaknesses**: we have limited experience with business decision-making, product management, sales, and customer-facing roles.

### Subject Matter Experts

Subject matter experts (SMEs) who can help us address our expertise gaps are
- **Business decision-makers:** Understand real-world decision-making workflows and customer needs
- **Domain experts:** Validate whether our AI usage make sense in specific industries

## Diversity

Our team has a varied background, but we acknowledge that we have limited diversity in:
1. **ethnicity. & cultural backgrounds** - all team members are Asian and can speak Mandarin
2. **small company working experience** - all team members worked in big company but startup
3. **academic fields** - all team members are mainly in CS & Statistics
### How this can impact us

A potential impact of this is
1. **Cultural blind spots**: We may make assumptions about how users from different cultural backgrounds communicate, work, or make business decisions
2. **Company-size blind spots**: We may design around the resources, data, and workflows of larger companies and overlook the constraints of small businesses
3. **Domain blind spots**: We may focus heavily on technical solutions without fully understanding the needs and workflows of our customers

People with backgrounds that differ from out own can help us.
