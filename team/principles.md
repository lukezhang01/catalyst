# Team Expectations & Working Principles

We expect every team member to contribute actively, communicate openly, and take ownership of their work. Our team values **trust, ownership, candor, and collaboration**.

### **1. Disagree Openly, Commit Fully**

* Everyone is expected to challenge decisions when they disagree and explain their reasoning.
* If disagreement remains, the Project Lead summarizes the options and makes the call.
* Once a decision is made, everyone commits fully and moves forward.

### **2. Own Your Commitments**

* Commitments live on the Project Board, with a clear owner and deadline.
* If you realize you cannot deliver, raise it early. The team adjusts the plan and agrees on a new commitment.
* Problems are surfaced, not personalized. We focus on solving problems rather than assigning blame.

### **3. Candid, Specific, Respectful**

* Give feedback directly and honestly to the person who can act on it.
* Critique the work, never the person.
* Do not hide feedback about someone's work. Withholding useful feedback denies them the opportunity to improve.

### **Communication**

* Communicate early when you are blocked, uncertain, or your plans change.
* Keep important decisions and commitments documented on the Project Board.
* Respect everyone's time while making sure important information reaches the people who need it.

### **Roles & Work Delegation**

* Every task has a clear owner who is responsible for driving it forward.
* Roles define primary ownership, not strict boundaries. Help outside your role when you can contribute.
* Delegate based on **skills, interest, and availability**, while giving owners enough context and autonomy to execute.

### **Team Value**

**Build together. Own your work. Make each other better.**
